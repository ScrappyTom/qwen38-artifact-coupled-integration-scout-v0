## The interaction result

### Ingress bandwidth changed both throughput and residency

Batching was genuinely used. I2 issued 11 multi-range batch actions and
admitted 25 ranges through 16 successful read actions. I4 issued seven
multi-range actions and admitted 19 ranges through 13 successful reads.

That bought source ranges per accepted actor decision, but larger exact results
also consumed residency faster. Both batch configurations reached the frozen
two-reentry ceiling. I2 then made three exact reopens; I4 acquired three.

The relevant relation is therefore not a context-free batching main effect:

```text
more exact evidence per accepted decision
                    ×
larger delivered result bodies
                    ↓
faster coverage, different turnover, earlier reentry pressure
```

### Bounded work generated meaning but did not retain it cumulatively

The maintenance path was active rather than merely syntactically qualified:

- I3 accepted 23 of 24 replacement records and spent 182,565 serialized
  maintenance tokens;
- I4 accepted 15 of 16 and spent 156,789;
- both produced grounded cross-source prose; and
- both cited 13 distinct source IDs at some point in their lifetimes.

The bounded replacement law then shed earlier grounding. I3's final 337-word
record cited only `S05`, `S06`, `S12`, and `S14` despite a 13-source mechanical
allowlist. I4's final 550-word record cited only `S06`, `S12`, `S13`, and `S14`
despite all 14 sources having crossed a model boundary. One I4 replacement
dropped every citation from its predecessor.

The growing source-ID wrapper was provenance metadata, not retained semantic
relationships. This separates four outcomes:

```text
maintenance expression        strongly activated
semantic interpretation       plainly present
cumulative semantic retention unstable
task-artifact utility          absent
```

### The sidecar was not an incremental task artifact

The frozen design called the work state "cumulative task-native work," but its
operational role was narrower: a lossy, non-authoritative replacement
evidence-integration sidecar. It was not a versioned charter section, claim
packet, requirement/evidence matrix with acceptance semantics, or partial
candidate that the ordinary actor could check and incrementally improve.

Consequently the actor still faced an all-or-nothing transition from evidence
plus sidecar to a complete 1,800–2,000-word charter replacement. That
transition never occurred. I4 is the strongest diagnostic case: all 14 sources
became model-visible, a substantial semantic record was resident, exact reopen
remained usable, and the actor still chose recovery rather than construction.

The system therefore preserved information flow and generated interpretation,
but did not bind interpretation to exact cumulative work, requirement
satisfaction, feedback, or the next admitted task action.

### Competing explanations remain live

The run does not identify one latent cause for nonconstruction. At least these
explanations remain compatible with the literal trajectory:

- the actor still judged additional exact evidence necessary;
- bounded replacement favored newly salient sources and displaced older
  relationships;
- the sidecar's non-authoritative role made it unsuitable as an action basis;
- complete-file replacement made the first artifact transaction too large or
  too committing relative to another read;
- rejected actions and maintenance calls consumed decision opportunity;
- the actor did not reliably use a model-authored derivative even when it was
  resident; or
- this model/task configuration could not perform the required integration
  within the frozen horizon regardless of the available surface.

The artifact-coupling successor is selected because it exposes several of
these relations in a downstream effect/check loop. It is not a post-hoc claim
that detached sidecar placement was the sole cause.

## What this changes

This result does not reopen receipt tuning, another note format, or a longer
run of the same configuration. It changes the system question:

```text

Observed failure

→ no cumulative task work

Next question
```

This is not a proposal to isolate and qualify three components separately. The
unit remains a complete trajectory in which ingress, pressure, interpretation,
artifact state, effects, checks, repair, and closure can alter one another.

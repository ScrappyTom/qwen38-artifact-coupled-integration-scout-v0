## Supported interpretation

The dedicated per-demotion 256-token source-residue interface did not qualify
reliable bootstrap under this task/model/runtime. The larger task-native
evidence-integration carrier qualified its two representative expression
shapes, but its downstream utility remains untested.

This is not evidence that semantic persistence is useless, that 310 tokens is
always uneconomic, or that cumulative work improves task performance. It is
evidence that the declared A/B/C/D comparison cannot be run faithfully through
its frozen transport.

## Failure migration and remaining system question

```text

        ↓

        ↓

```

E38 adds that per-source tiny semantic maintenance is not a qualified transport,
while task-native cumulative work expression is mechanically viable in the two
final shapes tested.

The next candidate interaction is therefore:

```text

×
cumulative task-native work externalization
```

A candidate four-configuration terrain scout would compare one-range versus
bounded multi-range acquisition and whole-final-artifact-only versus bounded
evidence-integration work. These are whole feedback systems: ingress affects
result size, pressure, and call budget; work affects residency, later demand,
construction, and anchoring risk.

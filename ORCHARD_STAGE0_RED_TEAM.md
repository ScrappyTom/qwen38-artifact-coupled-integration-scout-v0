## Principal risks

### Activation risk

The source world is large enough under one legal path, but realized model demand
may construct early, read narrower ranges, submit prematurely, or never approach
pressure. The screen qualifies only if at least ten sources and ten evidence
domains crossed model boundaries, the candidate is not submitted, and one
positive first-fit source substitution restores the pending packet.

### Transition reachability risk

The milestone is mechanically available but actor-invoked. Qwen may never call
`begin_verification`, may call it too early and consume rejection budget, or may
continue construction after the milestone. The host may not infer readiness or
force the phase change.

### Checker coverage risk

Expected/observed regex gates are more relational than concept presence, but
they remain bounded surface tests. They can false-negative permitted wording or
miss a novel relation error. External candidate-bound adjudication therefore
remains mandatory and actor-invisible.

### P1 recomposition cost

Offline counts show resident-token replacement, not cache reuse or prefill
economics. P1 may pay enough cache loss to erase its context advantage.

### Scaffold loss risk

The exact artifact may not have absorbed every useful relation when the
milestone fires. Demoting the scaffold can lower repair quality even while
improving capacity. This is a primary measured outcome, not an apparatus error.

### Compound-treatment interpretation

P1 jointly demotes the scaffold and replaces verification history. A positive
or negative result applies to that complete allocation in this configuration.
It cannot be attributed to one component without a later eligible ablation.

## Stop rules

The pressure screen stops without continuation if activation fails, custody or
runtime verification fails, the world is submitted, no source-externalizing
first-fit relief exists, or the exact screen cannot be sealed. The measured
runner is not frozen until a qualified fork has been independently audited.

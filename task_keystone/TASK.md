# Keystone Regional Rail Restoration Task

Produce two exact, source-grounded work products for the synthetic Keystone
regional rail network:

1. `EVIDENCE_INTEGRATION_LEDGER.md`, beginning exactly with
   `# Keystone Evidence and Decision Matrix`.
2. `BOUNDED_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE_DECISION.md`, beginning exactly with
   `# Keystone Regional Rail Restoration Decision` and containing exactly
   these ordered level-two headings:

   1. `Authority, scope, and operating states`
   2. `Track, route, and environmental restrictions`
   3. `Signals, cyber controls, and telemetry`
   4. `Traction power, fuel, and staged load`
   5. `Rolling stock, crews, and movement readiness`
   6. `Freight, passengers, accessibility, and communication`
   7. `Execution, rollback, and contingencies`
   8. `Verification, blockers, falsifiers, and closure`

The final decision must contain 1,400–1,900 words excluding bracketed source
citations and cite at least ten distinct exact source IDs. Reconcile authority,
track geometry, signal proving, traction power, rolling stock, staffing,
weather, fuel and spares, cyber controls, monitoring, freight restrictions,
passenger continuity, candidate lineage, and independent readiness.

Bind every action and gate to owners, prerequisites, observations, the current
candidate, falsifiers, rollback, and evidence that retires temporary controls.
Preserve observations versus gates, warning versus stop thresholds, installed
versus usable capacity, percent versus probability, hours versus days,
source-local authority, and current versus stale evidence.

Checks are candidate-bound mechanical feedback. They do not independently
authorize engineering movement, passenger service, or closure.

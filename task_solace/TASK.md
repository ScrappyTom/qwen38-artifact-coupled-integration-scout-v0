# Authoritative task — Solace regional-water recovery decision

Solace County must restore a regional drinking-water network after wildfire,
power, treatment, hydraulic, and control-system disturbances. Build and maintain
the exact candidate files, then propose submission only when the package is
genuinely ready for independent review.

The world contains fourteen exact, versioned source objects with opaque IDs.
Catalog titles help navigation but do not supply findings. Read the bytes needed
for the work and cite sources as `[SOURCE_ID]`.

## Required exact work

Maintain `EVIDENCE_INTEGRATION_LEDGER.md` as a compact task-native evidence and
decision matrix. Preserve source-bound facts, cross-source dependencies,
conflicts, unknowns, candidate currency, and citations. This exact artifact is
non-authoritative but may carry cumulative work across context turnover.

Construct `BOUNDED_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE_DECISION.md` as a 1,500–2,100-word Solace
Water Recovery Decision with exactly the declared section headings. Use at
least eleven distinct source IDs and integrate—not merely list—the governing
relationships.

Address all twelve requirement groups:

- Q01 — incident, utility, public-health, communication, and closure authority;
- Q02 — pressure, storage, isolation, residence time, and candidate-bound hydraulics;
- Q03 — sampling rounds, spacing, detection limits, action thresholds, and staleness;
- Q04 — pump capacity, shared-main limits, staged demand, and current evidence;
- Q05 — grid, generator, fuel, staffing, chemicals, and resource contingencies;
- Q06 — treatment barriers, turbidity, disinfectant, source blend, and contact time;
- Q07 — contamination hypotheses, observations, locations, investigation holds, and falsifiers;
- Q08 — access, token and key state, immutable custody, retention, and automation restoration;
- Q09 — telemetry coverage, warnings versus actions, delays, alternates, and uncertainty;
- Q10 — environmental determination, notice clock, permits, discharge, and evidence custody;
- Q11 — public advisories, accessibility, alternate water, service states, and communications;
- Q12 — candidate lineage, rollback, effect uptake, current checks, blockers, independent readiness, and closure.

## Relationship rules

Preserve psi versus kPa, gallons versus treated reserve, micrograms versus
milligrams, reporting limits versus action thresholds, percentages versus
measured concentration, rated versus usable power, nameplate versus shared
capacity, warnings versus isolation triggers, forecast versus observation, and
mechanical checks versus public-health and independent readiness. Candidate,
hydraulic, sampling, treatment, pump, power, key, role, telemetry, or source-
water changes can make earlier evidence stale.

The decision must state owners, inputs, resources, timing, dependencies,
effects, observations, contingencies, rollback and retirement conditions, and
evidence that would falsify important claims. Do not infer that a provenance
record, mutation, green check, or submission establishes readiness.

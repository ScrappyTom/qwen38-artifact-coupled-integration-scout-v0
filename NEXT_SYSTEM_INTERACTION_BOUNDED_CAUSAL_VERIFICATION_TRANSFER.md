## Research question

Can a complete bounded verification lifecycle preserve current state plus the
small amount of exact unresolved causality needed to convert rejected feedback
into a different admitted repair, current recheck, and correct closure?

The treatment unit is the whole trajectory:

```text
common exact E79 prefix

```

Source count, domain count, accessible-world size, first context pressure, and
host semantic readiness are not activation units.

## Exact event trigger

The common continuation may fork only after the authoritative trace contains:

1. a nontrivial candidate entering verification;
2. a current check bound to that candidate;
3. a rejected mutation with no candidate change;
4. a later exact observation acquired with no intervening candidate change;
   and
5. the next actor decision has not yet been made.

`reactive_runtime.causal_activation.detect_causal_fork_activation` owns this
mechanical decision. The host does not inspect evidence semantics, choose a
repair, or force the triggering sequence.

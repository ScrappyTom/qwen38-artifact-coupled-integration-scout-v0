# Orchard phase-lifecycle interaction result

Run ID: `2026-08-27-orchard-phase-lifecycle-measured-v0`

Frozen commit: `094bbce57407568d1ef0ecd94414ae1a957e3b45`

Configurations, in frozen order:

1. `F0_FIXED_SCAFFOLD_APPEND_ONLY_VERIFICATION`
2. `P1_PHASE_CONDITIONAL_CURRENT_VERIFICATION`

## Bottom line

The phase-conditional system produced a real lifecycle improvement but not a
useful completion.

Both configurations were identical through pressured acquisition, eight
semantic-maintenance calls, exact evidence-ledger construction, eight
section-sized decision mutations, and the actor-selected transition to
verification. Each entered verification with the same candidate and the same
twenty-claim provenance scaffold.

F0 retained that scaffold and appended ordinary verification history. It ran
one current check, recovered one two-source batch, and then stopped because the
next model-facing packet could not fit and no eligible exact result body could
restore feasibility. Its artifact remained a 1,075-word partial with nine
mechanical blockers.

P1 externalized the scaffold and rebuilt a bounded current verification frame
after each action. Its first verification prompt was 7,580 tokens rather than
F0's 19,010. It ran the same nine-blocker check, recovered defect-relevant exact
sources, made one admitted repair, received that effect in a later actor call,
and rechecked the current candidate. The recheck reduced nine mechanical
blockers to three. Direct source-bound review finds all twelve substantive
requirement groups present in the final P1 decision; the remaining true defects
are the 1,365-word decision falling 35 words short and the ledger using only
three of the eight required exact source-ID citations.

P1 then exposed the cost of aggressive current-state replacement. A second
patch was rejected because its anchor was non-unique. The actor next issued the
same `CURRENT:1-64` read four times, with byte-identical action content, until
the twelve-call verification budget expired. It never repaired the remaining
artifact-contract defects, obtained a passing check, or submitted.

The supported systems claim is:

> Phase-conditional scaffold demotion plus bounded mechanical current-state
> verification materially extended the feedback loop and improved exact
> artifact quality relative to fixed scaffold residency plus append-only
> verification. But replacement removed or failed to preserve enough recent
> repair/control continuity to prevent a deterministic repeat-read loop after
> action failure. The full P1 configuration improved operability and repair,
> not readiness or useful completion.

This is a compound configuration result. It does not isolate scaffold demotion
from current-state replacement.

## Common construction trajectory

The literal actor sequence was:

```text

read CULTURE + STERILE
replace exact evidence ledger
upsert eight exact decision sections

```

Before the continuation began, the frozen handoff had already delivered ten
sources under authentic prompt pressure. During the common continuation,
first-fit relief externalized eight result bodies. Every positive-savings
externalization triggered the same fallible anchored-provenance operation in
both cells.

The maintenance dispositions were also identical:

```text
global reject
partial admission
partial admission
full admission
full admission
full admission
partial admission
full admission
```

The bounded register reached twenty claims. The first maintenance output was
globally rejected for invalid claim count; later claims were individually or
transactionally admitted under the frozen provenance and budget rules. Rejected
maintenance content did not corrupt prior accepted state, and ordinary actor
work continued.

The actor began exact cumulative work early. It first created a thirteen-row
evidence matrix, then filled all eight required decision sections. This repeats
the Solace interaction at a fresh world: temporary semantic scaffolding,
task-native ledger state, section-sized actions, and exact artifact persistence
jointly supported admissible cumulative construction under pressure.

At actor call 12, both cells crossed the frozen construction milestone and
invoked `begin_verification` on exactly the same candidate hash:

`074c474d71067d91040b81bea2a3ae260ad0d7f0b34322dc2f97096688db7711`.

Only the post-transition information lifecycle differed.

## F0: fixed scaffold plus append-only verification

F0's verification sequence was:

```text
check current candidate
read CURRENT + ASSAY
context pressure without feasible relief
```

The first current check entered a 19,010-token prompt and exposed nine blockers:

* seven incomplete substantive groups;
* the decision word-count requirement; and
* ledger exact-citation breadth.

The actor requested `CURRENT` and `ASSAY`, a rational response to the utilities
and analytical failures. The next packet reached 20,607 tokens before delivery
of the new observation. No remaining eligible exact result body could restore
the required reserve, so the host stopped before another actor decision.

The final F0 artifact retained broad and mostly safe partial work, but no
verification repair occurred. Direct review classifies five substantive groups
as met and seven as partial. Its exact title, heading order, and decision source
breadth pass, but the decision is 325 words short and the ledger does not satisfy
the exact citation contract. F0 is `partial`, `not_ready`, and not a useful
completion.

This cell locally confirms the failure already exposed by Solace verification:
fixed semantic stock plus append-only check/action/effect chronology can make a
small useful current verification state unreachable.

## P1: phase-conditional current verification

P1's verification sequence was:

```text
check current candidate
read CHILL + CURRENT
read ASSAY
rejected patch: non-unique anchor
read CHILL
admitted patch
check repaired current candidate
rejected patch: non-unique anchor
read CURRENT
read CURRENT
read CURRENT
read CURRENT
verification-call budget exhausted
```

The phase transition externalized the register behind its exact handle and
recomposed the actor-facing state from the exact current candidate, candidate-
bound effect/check status, pending or latest update, source/history handles, and
the frozen verification contract. The first P1 check prompt was 11,430 tokens
smaller than F0's.

The first repair attempt failed mechanically because the requested patch anchor
was not unique. That failure did not mutate the candidate. After another exact
CHILL read, the second patch was admitted. It expanded the decision with:

* the cold-chain set point, allowed range, excursion, and stability blocker;
* WFI, power, generator, analytical, cyber, supply, observation, and
  communications relationships;
* additional exact source citations; and
* candidate-bound currentness language.

The repair effect crossed the next actor boundary. The actor then ran a check on
the repaired candidate, reducing the frozen mechanical blocker count from nine
to three.

The three reported blockers were:

1. one WFI relationship pattern;
2. decision word count; and
3. ledger source-citation breadth.

Direct review reconciles the WFI failure as a surface-order false negative. The
artifact says that WFI is 82 degrees Celsius and every return must remain at
least 80 degrees Celsius for three consecutive thirty-minute windows. The regex
requires the text `80 degrees` before `every return`; the meaning is intact.

P1 therefore ended with all twelve substantive requirement groups present and
no frozen reversal, but it still violated two exact work-product requirements.
It is `strong_partial`, `not_ready`, and not a useful completion.

## Why P1 stalled

The terminal four actions are the most important qualitative failure:

```text
read_source CURRENT 1-64
read_source CURRENT 1-64
read_source CURRENT 1-64
read_source CURRENT 1-64
```

Each prior read result entered the following actor decision. The action content
and output hash nevertheless remained identical. This was not a capacity stop:
the prompts remained roughly 9,300–11,100 tokens. It was a stable behavioral
loop inside an operable bounded frame.

The strongest explanation is an interaction, not a single broken component:

```text
current check still reports utility wording
+ second patch rejected on non-unique anchor
+ current-state replacement drops ordinary recent chronology
+ exact CURRENT bytes remain available
+ deterministic actor/seed sees nearly the same control situation
    -> repeat CURRENT demand
```

The trace proves the repeated state/action pattern. It does not prove which
omitted cue caused it. Plausible missing control information includes the exact
failed patch and rejection reason, already-attempted evidence recovery, or a
bounded causal tail showing that another identical read did not change the
candidate or check.

This is the mirror image of F0's failure:

```text
F0 preserves too much path
    -> current verification becomes physically unreachable

P1 preserves too little recent path/control
    -> current verification remains reachable but repeats
```

The next architecture cannot choose between permanent append-only history and a
memoryless current snapshot. It needs bounded replacement with explicit causal
continuity for the operations whose outcomes govern the next decision.

## System-level learning

### 1. The construction scaffold has phase-dependent value

The common trajectory provides a second fresh-world positive signal for the
complete scaffold-plus-ledger-plus-incremental-artifact construction system.
Keeping the same twenty-claim scaffold resident after construction did not help
F0 reach repair. Demoting it in P1 created enough space for a much deeper
verification loop.

This supports treating semantic residue as temporary construction scaffolding,
not permanent memory. It does not isolate or prove that scaffold demotion alone
caused the difference.

### 2. Mechanical current-state replacement transfers positively

Unlike scheduled low-pressure reentry, the Orchard transition occurred at an
actor-selected, mechanically qualified construction milestone against an active
verification-lifecycle problem. P1 did not merely survive longer: it consumed a
candidate-bound check, recovered targeted evidence, mutated exact work, observed
the mutation effect, and obtained a current recheck.

That is a stronger system result than capacity survival.

### 3. Current state is insufficient without bounded causal/control state

Replacing stale checks and old effects is necessary, but the replacement frame
must preserve enough exact recent causality to distinguish:

* a source not yet consulted from one just consulted repeatedly;
* a repair not attempted from a rejected repair;
* an unchanged check from a new current check; and
* continued evidence demand from failure to convert evidence into work.

This need not be a freeform semantic progress note. The host can mechanically
bind the latest admitted/rejected action, result/effect, candidate version,
check currency, and exact repetition count. Whether that bounded causal tail is
sufficient remains untested.

### 4. Action binding is part of lifecycle economics

Two of P1's twelve verification calls were lost to non-unique patch anchors.
The second rejection preceded the repeat-read loop. Exact current-state
management cannot compensate for a repair surface whose actions are difficult
to bind reliably. Repair transport, causal-state replacement, and model demand
must be evaluated together.

### 5. Completion remains the governing endpoint

P1's stronger artifact and deeper loop are meaningful, but neither arm passed
the frozen evaluator, submitted, or achieved independently ready closure.
Longer operability, fewer blockers, and substantive repair are intermediate
system outcomes—not useful completion.

## Recommended next program question

Do not tune Orchard's prompt, add more calls, or rerun this donor.

The new interaction boundary is prospective transfer of a bounded verification
frame that combines:

* exact current artifact and candidate identity;
* latest admitted effect and current check projection;
* scaffold demotion after task-native construction;
* a small mechanically derived causal tail containing the latest action,
  rejection/result, and recurrence facts;
* a repair action with unique structural binding; and
* exact handles to full history and externalized evidence.

The comparison should remain a complete lifecycle system, not an isolated
causal-tail field. It should continue through repair, effect uptake, current
recheck, readiness, and closure. A fresh task/world is required before another
confirmatory GPU study.

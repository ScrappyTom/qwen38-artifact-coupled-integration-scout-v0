# Task: Bluehaven drinking-water restoration package

A river contamination event, power instability, and distribution constraints
threaten Bluehaven's drinking-water system. Produce two exact deliverables:

1. `EVIDENCE_INTEGRATION_LEDGER.md`
2. `BOUNDED_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE_DECISION.md`

The fixed second filename is imposed by the runtime; its content is an
operational drinking-water restoration decision. Reconcile the complete system
over seventy-two hours rather than summarizing sources independently.

## Evidence-ledger contract

Begin with `# Evidence Integration Ledger` and maintain entries `R01` through
`R12`. Each entry must state a disposition, cite only exact observed sources as
`[SNN]`, preserve units, probabilities, revisions, conflicts and
qualifications, bind current or missing evidence, and name blockers. The ledger
is model-authored, lossy task work and never readiness authority.

1. `## Decision, scope, and authority`

6. `## Public warning, sampling, and accountability`
7. `## Seventy-two-hour execution and contingencies`
8. `## Verification, readiness, blockers, and falsifiers`

Write 1,400–2,000 words excluding exact citations. Cite at least twelve unique
sources and include S01–S11 and S13–S16.

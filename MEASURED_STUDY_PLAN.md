# Northstar artifact-coupling measured study plan

Status: closed without measurement. The authorized Northstar screen reached
pressure before its frozen meaningful-acquisition threshold, so no qualifying
handoff exists and the D0/A1 continuation remains ineligible.

## System question

At one authentic result-delivery pressure boundary, does coupling bounded
source integration to an exact, revisable task artifact improve the complete
evidence-to-work loop relative to the same integration maintained as a detached
model-facing sidecar?

The study keeps ingress, custody, relief, maintenance, actor work, candidate
effects, checks, repair, readiness, and closure active. It does not score the
maintenance artifact or edit action as an isolated component.

## Common pre-treatment fork

The fork must be generated by
`2026-08-24-northstar-transfer-pressure-screen-v0`. It is not yet available.
The exact handoff must prove:

- a pending undelivered source observation;
- at least four previously delivered source observations;
- an unchanged initial candidate;
- no prior check or submission;
- ordinary prospective prompt pressure above 20,992 tokens; and
- at least one strictly positive first-fit relief action with the pending result
  protected.

Both arms hydrate the same exact messages, result ledger, candidate, pending
result, and task/world. The screen contains no semantic maintenance or
treatment effect.

## Common interacting system

At pressure, the host scans previously visible exact source results in frozen
oldest-first order. It prospectively renders each receipt, skips non-positive
substitutions, applies at most the first strictly positive substitution, and
rerenders the complete packet. The pending result cannot be demoted.

Each actual source externalization invokes bounded integration maintenance over
only:

- the authoritative task;
- the prior accepted integration, if any; and
- the newly externalized exact observation.

The source allowlist is mechanically derived from evidence already delivered
to that arm. Maintenance cannot create readiness authority or introduce a
catalog-only source.

The ordinary actor can read or batch-read, reopen exact external results,
replace the ledger, revise decision sections, replace the decision, run a
candidate-bound check, and submit. Candidate effects and checks must cross a
later actor boundary to count as uptake.

Reactive reentry is allowed only if a pressured packet has no strictly positive
eligible relief. It may include exact task/current candidate, actual arm state,
undelivered updates, a mechanical history directory, and exact reopen handles;
it may not add post-hoc relevant evidence.

## Causal contrast

`D0_DETACHED` stores each accepted complete replacement ledger as a
non-authoritative model-facing sidecar. Maintenance does not mutate either
required candidate file.

`A1_COUPLED` writes the same accepted bytes to the exact versioned
`EVIDENCE_INTEGRATION_LEDGER.md` candidate file. It emits a candidate effect,
can make prior checks stale, and supplies the prior artifact for later
maintenance. The actor may revise or replace it.

Once behavior diverges, reads, effects, maintenance inputs, pressure, costs,
and artifacts may diverge endogenously. Those are system outcomes.

## Resource contract

Per configuration:

- up to 22 preconstruction actor calls;
- exactly eight post-milestone actor calls;
- at most 30 actor calls total;
- at most 16 maintenance calls;
- at most two reactive reentries;
- at most 1,400,000 serialized tokens;
- at most 10,800 wall-clock seconds;
- one attempt per call and zero retries.

The two-arm maximum is 60 actor calls, 32 maintenance calls, and 92 provider
calls. A harmful first arm does not cancel the second. Apparatus, custody, or
runtime failure stops without retry.

## Outcomes

Primary outcomes are reported separately:

- exact candidate-bound artifact quality;
- readiness and closure correctness;
- effect uptake;
- useful completion.

Useful completion requires complete quality, correct closure, and the final
relevant effect crossing a model boundary or being independently reconciled.

Secondary outcomes include evidence conversion, maintenance acceptance and
source coverage, construction timing, postconstruction actions, reads,
reopens, relief, reentry, mutations, checks, repairs, submissions, prompt and
completion tokens, cache reuse, latency, anchoring, interference, and failure
migration.

This is a compound configuration comparison in one fresh world. A positive
result does not identify a universally useful component or promote a general
architecture. A negative result does not automatically reopen prompt-format
tuning; its first materially new interaction failure determines any successor.

# Measured artifact-coupled interaction plan

## Why this is a system experiment

The authentic pressure screen did not merely qualify a maintenance component.
It froze a live evidence-to-work pipeline at the point where a newly acquired
exact observation cannot reach another actor decision. The measured study
continues that same task and keeps every relevant function active:

`evidence ingress -> exact delivery -> pressure relief -> semantic integration`
`-> actor uptake -> task-artifact mutation -> effect uptake -> check -> repair`
`-> closure or budget exhaustion`

The unit of analysis is the resulting trajectory. Integration expression,
eviction, editing, and checking are not scored as isolated wins. A system can
save context yet harm work, produce a good semantic ledger that never affects
the candidate, couple a ledger into the candidate but anchor an error, or
reduce reads while causing false closure. Those are interaction outcomes.

## Matched configurations

Both configurations use the same task, exact sources, boundary history,
candidate, actor actions, model/runtime/sampler, actor seed, maintenance
instruction, maintenance seed, 1,600-token integration budget, pressure rule,
reopen mechanism, reentry rule, evaluator, and resource ceilings.

`D0_DETACHED` keeps each accepted complete-replacement integration ledger as a
non-authoritative model-facing sidecar. Maintenance produces a semantic-state
effect but does not change either required task file.

`A1_COUPLED` writes the accepted bytes to the exact versioned
`EVIDENCE_INTEGRATION_LEDGER.md` candidate file. It therefore produces a
candidate effect, can alter subsequent checks, can be revised by ordinary actor
actions, and becomes the prior work product for later maintenance.

This feedback difference is the treatment. Once trajectories diverge,
actor-requested reads, mutations, maintenance inputs, pressure events, and
costs may also diverge endogenously.

## Pressure and maintenance loop

When a complete prospective actor packet exceeds 20,992 tokens, the host scans
previously visible exact evidence results in frozen oldest-first order. For
each candidate it renders the actual receipt in the complete packet. It skips
non-positive substitutions and applies the first positive one, then stops that
pass. The undelivered result is protected.

Every actual evidence externalization triggers one dedicated integration call.
That call receives only:

- the authoritative task;
- the prior accepted bounded integration state, if any; and
- the exact result just externalized.

Its source allowlist is mechanically limited to sources already represented in
that prior plus the newly externalized result. In A1, an actor revision to the
coupled ledger can enter the next maintenance prior only for cited source IDs
that had already crossed an actor boundary. No catalog-only source becomes
evidence.

The complete packet is rerendered after the receipt, maintenance output,
semantic-state or candidate effect, and current-state replacement. If still
over limit, the loop repeats. This measures production cost, resident cost,
candidate effects, induced demand, cache loss, and failure migration together.

## Reentry and exact recovery

Reentry is reactive, not scheduled. It is allowed only when the packet is over
limit and no strictly positive eligible evidence substitution remains. At most
two reentries are allowed per configuration.

The fresh packet contains the exact task and action contract, exact current
candidate, the integration state in its actual arm-specific location, a
mechanical exact-history directory, and every undelivered update. Previously
delivered exact history remains externally reopenable. Reentry cannot inject a
source that the actor never acquired.

## Resource contract

Each configuration receives at most:

- 20 continuation actor calls;
- 12 integration-maintenance calls;
- 32 provider calls total;
- 1,000,000 serialized tokens;
- 7,200 wall-clock seconds;
- one attempt per call and zero retries.

The two-cell run therefore permits at most 40 actor calls, 24 maintenance
calls, and 64 provider calls. A poor or harmful first cell does not cancel the
second. An apparatus, custody, or runtime failure stops the aggregate without a
retry.

## Evaluation

Literal behavior and mechanics are recorded first: delivery, substitutions,
maintenance acceptance, state/candidate effects, effect uptake, actions,
checks, repair, submission, tokens, caching, latency, and reentry.

The external evaluator then binds its result to the exact composite candidate.
A mechanical precheck is not readiness authority. Final artifact quality and
closure require condition-blinded semantic adjudication. Immediate submission
can therefore be a false-closure loss rather than an efficiency win.

## Interpretation

A coupled win would mean the **complete A1 configuration** had positive local
value at this boundary. It would not prove that artifact coupling alone caused
every difference or that it transfers.

A detached win could indicate that coupling anchored error, consumed too much
candidate/prompt budget, or distorted later control. A tie with different
costs remains informative. Nonactivation, maintenance failure, reentry,
reopening, and budget exhaustion are system outcomes rather than exclusions
unless an apparatus invariant fails.

The study stops after this matched comparison and external adjudication. It
does not automatically tune the maintenance prompt or artifact format. Any
successor must be selected from the first materially new interaction failure.

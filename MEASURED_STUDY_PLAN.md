# Cedar artifact-coupling measured study plan

Status: authentic Cedar pressure boundary qualified; measured GPU execution withheld pending exact result commit and separate authorization.

## System question

After a fresh actor has acquired materially broad evidence and a novel exact
observation creates real result-delivery pressure, does coupling bounded
source integration to exact revisable work improve the complete evidence-to-
artifact loop relative to the same integration maintained as a detached
model-facing sidecar?

This is a whole-system interaction study. Both arms retain ingress, exact
custody, relief, integration maintenance, actor work, candidate effects,
candidate-bound checks, repair, readiness, and closure.

## Common fork and evidence parity

The screen runs ordinary chronology only. A fork qualifies from exact delivered
line coverage—not result packaging—after four sources across three domains are
mature and a novel pending source result overflows. The candidate must remain
initial and no check or submission may have occurred. Offline audit must prove
positive protected-pending first-fit relief.

Both arms hydrate the same exact messages, result ledger, pending result,
candidate, and task/world. After behavior diverges, requested evidence and
effects may diverge endogenously. Neither reentry nor maintenance may inject a
catalog-only source or investigator-selected useful evidence.

## Shared interacting system

At pressure the host applies at most the first strictly positive receipt
substitution in frozen order, rerendering after every step and protecting the
pending result. Each actual source externalization invokes complete-replacement
integration maintenance over only the task, prior accepted integration, and
the newly externalized exact observation. Its source allowlist derives from
evidence already delivered to that arm.

The actor can continue single/batch reads, exact reopens, ledger replacement,
section upserts, full decision replacement, current candidate checks, repair,
and submission. Effects and observations count as uptake only after crossing a
later actor boundary. Reactive exact reentry is available only when positive
relief is exhausted and cannot add semantic evidence.

## Causal contrast

`D0_DETACHED` keeps accepted integration as a non-authoritative sidecar.

`A1_COUPLED` writes the same accepted bytes into the exact versioned required
ledger and emits a candidate effect. The actor may revise it. Maintenance is
neither authoritative nor immutable, and any candidate change can stale checks.

## Resource and outcome contract

Each configuration has 26 preconstruction calls, eight postconstruction calls,
18 maintenance calls, two reactive reentries, 1.6 million serialized tokens,
10,800 seconds, one attempt per call, and zero retries. Both arms run even if
the first is harmful unless apparatus or safety invalidates continuation.

Primary outcomes remain separate: exact artifact quality, closure correctness,
effect uptake, and useful completion. Useful completion requires complete
semantic quality, ready status under the frozen protocol, appropriate closure,
and final-effect uptake or independent reconciliation. Secondary outcomes
include evidence coverage, semantic maintenance, candidate work, reads/reopens,
relief/reentry, checks/repairs, cache and latency cost, anchoring, interference,
and failure migration.

A result supports one configuration-level interaction claim in one fresh world.
It neither isolates a component nor promotes a general architecture.

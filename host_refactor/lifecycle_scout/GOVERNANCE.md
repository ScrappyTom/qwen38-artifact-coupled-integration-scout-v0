# E97 Verification-Lifecycle Scout Governance

## Objective

Observe whether the integrated Trellis system can move one exact pending candidate effect into a model decision, remain oriented after mechanically compacting older applied action/effect pairs, enter verification, repair the independently frozen defects, recheck the changed candidate, and stop correctly.

## Scientific identity

This is a donor-derived descriptive lifecycle scout. It is not a fresh-world transfer and it does not isolate why the earlier semantic-scaffold configuration induced construction.

The imported state is the exact E96 treatment state immediately before its host recorded `capacity_blocked`. The historical checkpoint remains immutable. Migration excludes only that terminal event, records its exact bytes and hash, binds the exact donor checkpoint and candidate, preserves cumulative call and token costs, and creates a new checkpoint under the E97 execution manifest.

## Frozen system

- exact external custody and ordinary actor demand;
- deterministic first-positive pressure relief;
- the existing lossy temporary provenance scaffold;
- exact evidence ledger and exact incremental candidate;
- E97 applied action/effect compaction after delivery and lineage proof;
- the exact pending candidate effect and current-candidate effect slot;
- candidate-bound check, bounded section repair, current recheck, and submit proposal;
- exact raw evaluator custody with a compact candidate-bound actor projection containing failed criterion IDs, short descriptions, and frozen expectations;
- one possible remaining maintenance call only if ordinary pressure relief externalizes a new source result during construction;
- mandatory human review after each six additional actor calls.

No new note, digest, readiness cue, semantic repair, action-history summary, or automatic loop intervention may be added during the run.

## Success and failure

Packet feasibility is apparatus qualification, not success. The scout records:

1. delivery of the pending effect through a completed request;
2. the actor's next action and orientation to the current candidate;
3. construction completion or transition to verification;
4. current check findings;
5. targeted candidate-changing repair;
6. delivery of that effect and current recheck;
7. correct submission only when candidate-bound readiness permits it, or an explicit incomplete trajectory;
8. exact reopens, repeat demand, recurrence, and total marginal plus inherited cost.

A positive donor result earns fresh-world transfer. A negative result routes only from its first new transcript-level failure boundary.

## Stop rules

- one attempt per provider call and zero retries;
- no automatic continuation after a six-call checkpoint;
- provider, capacity, binding, domain, token-budget, or call-budget terminal states are sealed as observed outcomes;
- a policy change requires a new experiment and new authorization;
- no GPU or live model call occurs without authorization bound to the exact frozen commit.

# Aster Stage 0 red-team audit

Date: 2026-08-25

Status: offline review passed with named residual risks; no live inference

## Whole-system and oracle audit

- Aster is fresh relative to the architecture, wildfire, water, infusion, and prior code worlds.
- Source IDs are opaque and do not encode requirement ordinals.
- The gold requirement-to-source graph is evaluator-only.
- Both configurations share exact world, ingress, task work, effects, checks, repair/recheck, actor budget, and external adjudication.
- L1 receives only source observations externalized by its own common mechanical trajectory.
- The maintenance provider receives no hidden gold map or post-outcome evidence selection.
- W0 remains viable task work, not a no-relief or receipt-only death control.

Residual risk: titles reveal broad source topics, as they would in an ordinary
catalog. They do not reveal findings, correct cross-source predicates, or the
gold requirement graph.

## Provenance and semantic-authority audit

- The model chooses owner, result, one exact evidence-line quote, mode, referents, and statement.
- The host uniquely resolves the quote inside the delivered range and derives line number and span hash; the model counts no unnumbered lines and calculates no cryptographic identity.
- Source-slot mutation cannot create or update a referent's slot.
- Relationship referents must occur in the exact owner span.
- Multi-source synthesis is excluded from the maintenance carrier and remains exact actor work.
- Closure language and authoritative derivative claims are rejected.
- One current register message is replaced rather than appended.

Residual risk: mechanical provenance cannot detect predicate, modality, unit,
authority, or qualification reversal. The one live expression gate includes a
frozen material-safety review, and final artifact quality remains primary.

## Activation audit

- Eligibility depends on realized prompt pressure and actual first-fit source externalization.
- Candidate construction and checks before pressure remain eligible and exactly bound.
- Submission before pressure terminates the screen.
- The prospective path uses only legal full-pair actions and pressures at step six.
- All full single and pair observations fit their per-result ingress limits.

Residual risk: the live actor may complete or submit before pressure, choose a
low-materialization path, or reach pressure whose relief selects no source.
Those are structural nonactivation outcomes, not permission to alter the task.

## Resource audit

- Every prospective maintenance packet fits its 1,800-token completion allowance.
- The maximum one-claim-per-source register is 4,377 tokens; the hard stock ceiling is 8,000.
- L1 maintenance calls are additional and separately counted.
- Both arms retain twelve protected postconstruction actor decisions.
- Call, serialized-token, wall, response, and maintenance ceilings are frozen.

Residual risk: the treatment register can itself induce prompt pressure, lost
cache reuse, neighboring exact demand, or semantic anchoring. Those are measured
system costs, not apparatus failures.

## Evaluation and readiness audit

- Checks bind the exact two-file candidate hash.
- Candidate effects stale prior checks.
- Mechanical checks return only `not_ready` or `not_adjudicated`.
- The semantic rubric is criterion-level, source-bound, and candidate-bound.
- Readiness requires all blocking criteria, current verification, appropriate closure, and independent adjudication.
- The treatment register is not quality evidence unless its content also appears correctly in exact task work.

Residual risk: mechanical term gates are incomplete and can be gamed. They
exist to exercise feedback and frozen traps; condition-blinded semantic review
governs useful completion.

## Disposition

The task, exact source lock, relation topology, ingress geometry, provenance
carrier, host-derived evidence binding, bounded register lifecycle, exact task
work, effect/currentness loop, evaluator, pressure runner, budgets, and stopping
rules qualify offline. No provider call follows without a committed freeze and
explicit user authorization.

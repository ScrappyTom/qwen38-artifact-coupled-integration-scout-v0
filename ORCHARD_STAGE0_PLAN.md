## Whole-system question

Can a bounded actor use provenance-grounded semantic scaffolding while exact
evidence is being converted into exact incremental work, then demote that
scaffold and replace stale verification history with a bounded mechanical
current-state projection strongly enough to reach repair, recheck, readiness,
and correct closure?

This is not a component-isolation study. The compared systems share the same
complete acquisition/construction configuration and differ only after the same
mechanical construction milestone:

```text
F0
  anchored scaffold retained
  + exact incremental artifact
  + append-only verification action/check/effect history

P1
  same construction system
  -> scaffold externalized at the frozen milestone
  + exact incremental artifact
  + replace-in-place current verification projection
```

No outcome will isolate scaffold demotion from verification-state replacement.
That coupling is intentional; component ablation becomes eligible only after a
fresh-world whole-lifecycle signal.

## Fresh task

Orchard is a deterministic synthetic biologics-restart world with thirteen
materially interacting sources. Its evaluator scores relationships involving:

* stabilization, lot-release, commercial-restart, and closure authority;
* observed yield versus a two-batch gate;
* aseptic locations, units, and alert/action limits;
* signed cold-chain ranges and excursion disposition;
* every-return utility gates, installed/usable power, and generator duration;
* analytical observations, specifications, current limits, and supersession;
* access authority, key state, and retention periods;
* stock measures, operating regimes, staffing times, and uncertainty;
* determination-triggered environmental clocks and stop thresholds;
* monitoring coverage, warning/stop semantics, and percentile latency;
* communication coverage, state vocabulary, and capacity;
* candidate lineage, stale evidence, independent review, and acceptance.

The exact artifacts are a source-grounded evidence matrix and a 1,400–1,900
word, eight-section restart decision. Bounded section and patch actions are
available in both arms.

## Prospective phase transition

The actor may invoke `begin_verification` only when the exact decision has the
frozen title and headings, at least 900 words, and at least seven cited source
IDs. The host tests those mechanical conditions. The event changes phase but
does not assert semantic quality, readiness, or closure.

## P1 current-verification projection

After the transition and after every verification action, P1 replaces its old
model-facing verification state with:

* exact current candidate and version;
* latest admitted effect and whether it is pending or delivered;
* one current check, one stale check binding, or explicit not-yet-checked state;
* exact expected/observed blocking relations;
* exact result, semantic-scaffold, and full-history handles.

The full event ledger remains append-only and exact outside context. The host
does not semantically summarize or repair it.

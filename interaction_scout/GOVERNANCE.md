# Whole-system interaction governance

## Research unit

The unit is the complete configuration over time:

```text
exact world and custody
→ actor-selected evidence ingress
→ deterministic pressure relief
→ optional charged semantic maintenance
→ exact incremental task artifact
→ candidate effects and currentness
→ check, repair, recheck, and closure proposal
```

No result may attribute a behavioral difference to the semantic scaffold
alone. Maintenance recency, scaffold state, candidate growth, action
granularity, later packet composition, and model demand move together.

## Ownership

The host owns only mechanical facts: exact identities and bytes, source and
candidate versions, acquired versus delivered status, residency, token counts,
relief, request exposure, effect/check binding, budgets, and checkpoints.

The actor chooses task actions. The maintenance model chooses bounded meaning
from newly externalized sources. Its output is non-authoritative, admitted per
claim, and cannot authorize readiness or closure.

Codex reviews literal transcripts at each twelve-call checkpoint. It may
recommend an unchanged continuation, stop, or a new experiment. It may not
silently alter the active policy.

## Safety and stopping

- Fresh server process per configuration.
- One attempt per provider call and zero retries.
- Exact authorization bounds actor, maintenance, provider, and token totals.
- Invalid maintenance output consumes cost and leaves prior state unchanged.
- Scaffold bodies are demoted when verification begins.
- External candidate evaluation is identical across arms and not actor-visible.
- A policy, prompt, action-surface, or budget change creates a new experiment.
- GPU execution requires a clean exact commit and explicit authorization.

## Review questions

At every checkpoint record, in plain language:

1. What exact evidence, semantic residue, artifact state, and feedback was visible?
2. What did the actor ask for, and was that demand new, repeated, or displaced?
3. What exact work changed, and which visible information entered or disappeared?
4. Did candidate effects and checks enter a later completed decision?
5. Is acquisition, construction, or repair making progress, or repeating without it?
6. What is the next bottleneck in the whole configuration?

These are investigator judgments, not automatic host triggers.

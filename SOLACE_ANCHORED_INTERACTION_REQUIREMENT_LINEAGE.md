## Requirement-level interpretation constraints

- A source can be visible in the ledger or the actor prompt without being a register claim. For example, L1’s final Q11 and Q12 surface can be present even though M06/M07 admission records reject those claims.
- Register rejection is a mechanical fact. Treating it as the cause of a later Q-partial score would be an inference; the evidence also supports alternative explanations including section-heading construction, global-consolidation timing, omitted checking, evaluator’s literal term gates, and action-budget pressure.
- W0 A17/A18 are relevant counterevidence to a “no construction attempt” story. Their broad visible content can be compared against Q01–Q12 only as **unadmitted, truncated draft text**. The candidate evaluator correctly scores W0 from its unchanged placeholder, not from those outputs.
- The shared terminal `not_ready` disposition remains controlling for lifecycle claims. Q-level semantic coverage in L1 is not independent readiness or closure.

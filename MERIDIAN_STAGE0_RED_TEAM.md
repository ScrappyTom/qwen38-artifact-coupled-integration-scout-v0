# Meridian Stage 0 red-team audit

Date: 2026-08-25

Status: offline review passed with named residual risks; no live inference

## Oracle and parity audit

- Source IDs are opaque words and do not share requirement ordinals.
- The gold requirement-to-source graph is evaluator-only.
- Both configurations have the same exact world, catalog, actor-selected
  observations, response reserve, pressure rule, decision actions, effect
  binding, checks, repair/recheck surface, actor budget, and external evaluator.
- L1 receives only observations selected by its own inherited actor trajectory
  and externalized by the common mechanical rule.
- W0 receives no hidden gold map; its additional action only writes one slot
  bound to an exact source ID and SHA-256.
- W0 may write that slot only after a matching exact source/version result has
  crossed its own actor boundary; result IDs are attached mechanically.
- L1 maintenance cannot introduce investigator-selected unread evidence.

## Semantic authority audit

- A delta cannot contain a complete register or source-slot control markers.
- It must cover exactly the current batch and may mention no other known source.
- Each block carries the exact source SHA-256 and fixed local headings.
- The host merges mechanically and never combines claims semantically.
- Prior content from unrelated slots is not sent to the maintenance call and
  remains byte-identical.
- W0 and L1 use the same exact tokenizer and 650-token per-source slot budget.
- Closure and submission language are rejected.

Residual risk: exact source provenance does not establish semantic correctness.
A fluent delta can invert a relationship within its allowed source. The final
artifact evaluator and independent adjudication therefore remain primary.

## Resource audit

- All full singles and pairs are admissible.
- Every prospective delta packet leaves more than 18,700 tokens after its
  1,800-token completion allowance.
- Maintenance is separately charged and capped at twelve calls.
- Each arm retains a ten-call postconstruction tail.
- The screen stops rather than continuing if construction/check/closure occurs
  before pressure, because that trajectory does not expose the selected fork.

Residual risk: the actor may follow a source path that never pressures or may
construct early. Such a valid screen result is structural nonactivation, not a
license to alter the task.

## Evaluation and readiness audit

- Checks bind the exact two-file candidate hash.
- Candidate effects stale earlier checks.
- Mechanical checks can return only `not_ready` or `not_adjudicated`.
- Semantic readiness is independently candidate/task/rubric/evidence-bound.
- The evaluator includes unit, probability, coverage, shared-capacity,
  target/observation, temperature, currentness, and self-authorization traps.

Residual risk: mechanical term gates are not a semantic quality score. They
exist to make repair/currentness reachable and to catch exact frozen errors;
condition-blinded criterion-level adjudication governs useful completion.

## Apparatus disposition

The offline task, source lock, ingress enumeration, local-delta validator,
source-slot merge, direct-work action, effect/currentness loop, evaluator,
pressure runner, budgets, and stop rules qualify for a common pressure screen.
The deterministic Meridian source generator is self-contained; rebuilding the
catalog and custody lock reproduced the same lock SHA-256 exactly.
No provider call is implied by this audit.

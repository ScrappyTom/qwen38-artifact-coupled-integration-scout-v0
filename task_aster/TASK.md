# Authoritative task — Aster multi-region payment recovery decision

Aster Payments must recover a multi-region transaction platform after ledger,
queue, access, and external-rail disturbances. Build and maintain the exact
candidate files, then propose submission only when the package is genuinely
ready for independent review.

The world contains sixteen exact, versioned source objects with opaque IDs.
Catalog titles help navigation but do not supply findings. Read the bytes needed
for the work and cite sources as `[SOURCE_ID]`.

## Required exact work

Maintain `EVIDENCE_INTEGRATION_LEDGER.md` as a compact task-native evidence and
decision matrix. Preserve established source-bound facts, explicit
cross-source dependencies, conflicts, unknowns, candidate currency, and the
exact source citations needed to review them. This work is non-authoritative.

Construct `BOUNDED_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE_DECISION.md` as a 1,500–2,100-word Aster
Payment Recovery Decision with exactly the declared section headings. Use at
least twelve distinct source IDs and integrate—not merely list—the governing
relationships.

Address all twelve requirement groups:

- Q01 — authority for isolation, repair, verification, traffic restoration, and closure;
- Q02 — ledger replication, consistency, RPO/RTO, schema, writer, and settlement bindings;
- Q03 — idempotency scopes, retry windows, duplicate controls, and queue identity;
- Q04 — queue drain, event ordering, traffic interaction, pause/resume, and retirement;
- Q05 — shared capacity, staged traffic ramp, dependency headroom, and current load evidence;
- Q06 — settlement cutoff, prefunding, rail fallback, finality, and same-day contingencies;
- Q07 — fraud thresholds, manual review, customer state, communications, and privacy;
- Q08 — key/token status, break-glass access, audit integrity, and retention;
- Q09 — regulatory determination, reporting clock, aggregate loss, and evidence custody;
- Q10 — telemetry coverage, alerts versus holds, sampling, reconciliation, and uncertainty;
- Q11 — candidate lineage, rollback compatibility, effect uptake, current check, repair, and recheck;
- Q12 — blockers, falsifiers, independent readiness, accepted residual risk, and closure evidence.

## Relationship rules

Preserve milliseconds versus seconds, transactions per second versus per
minute, percentages versus probabilities, availability versus payment success,
targets versus observations, component ratings versus shared usable capacity,
sample coverage versus loss rate, and mechanical checks versus independent
readiness. A candidate, schema, writer route, queue policy, service limit, key
set, role, rail route, settlement cutoff, or sample-plan effect can make earlier
evidence stale.

The decision must state owners, inputs, resources, timing, dependencies,
effects, observations, contingencies, rollback and retirement conditions, and
evidence that would falsify important claims. Do not infer that a green format
check, provenance record, mutation, or submission establishes readiness.

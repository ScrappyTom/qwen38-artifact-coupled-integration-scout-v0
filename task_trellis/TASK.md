# Trellis Urban Heat Continuity Task

Produce two exact, source-grounded work products for the synthetic Trellis
urban heat emergency:

1. `EVIDENCE_INTEGRATION_LEDGER.md`, beginning exactly with
   `# Trellis Evidence-to-Requirement Matrix`.
2. `BOUNDED_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE_DECISION.md`, beginning exactly with
   `# Trellis Urban Heat Continuity Decision` and containing exactly these
   ordered level-two headings:

   1. `Authority, scope, and operating states`
   2. `Heat triggers and geographic staging`
   3. `Power, water, and cooling continuity`
   4. `Clinical, shelter, and accessibility operations`
   5. `Transit, communications, logistics, and staffing`
   6. `Execution, rollback, verification, and closure`

The final decision must contain 1,200–1,650 words excluding bracketed source
citations and cite at least ten distinct exact source IDs. Reconcile authority,
hazard thresholds, power, water, clinical surge, cooling-center capacity,
accessible transit, public communication, logistics, staffing, candidate
lineage, and independent readiness.

Bind each action and gate to owners, prerequisites, observations, the current
candidate, falsifiers, rollback, and evidence that retires temporary controls.
Preserve observations versus gates, probability versus coverage, installed
versus usable capacity, reserve versus flow, median versus percentile, hours
versus days, source-local authority, and current versus stale evidence.

Checks are candidate-bound mechanical feedback. They do not independently
authorize operations, readiness, submission, or closure.

## Why this is the next unit

The live host smoke qualified one pressured decision but did not exercise the
writable candidate, verification, repair, semantic-scaffold, or resume paths.
The next unit therefore uses the refactored host as common infrastructure and
tests a complete information-to-work interaction. It is not another host seam
test and not a scaffold-expression test.

The task is a fresh trajectory on the already frozen Trellis world. Reusing the
task keeps the task adapter, evaluator, and action meanings stable while the
new event host is exercised from an initial canonical candidate state. This is
not cross-world transfer; a positive signal would still require that later.

## Compared configurations

`V0_EXACT_ARTIFACT` receives exact evidence, common pressure relief and reopen,
an exact evidence ledger, incremental decision sections, exact candidate
effects, candidate-bound checks, bounded repair, recheck, and submission.

`V1_TEMPORARY_PROVENANCE_SCAFFOLD` receives the same system. During
construction only, a separately charged maintenance call may turn newly
externalized exact source results into individually admitted, anchored,
non-authoritative claims. Valid claims enter a replaceable state slot. Invalid
claims are discarded without repair or retry. When verification begins, the
claim body is replaced by a compact external-custody receipt; it cannot
authorize readiness or closure.

This is a whole-configuration contrast. Maintenance exposure, scaffold state,
artifact growth, action granularity, later prompt composition, and model demand
can all interact. The experiment will not describe the scaffold alone as the
cause of any downstream difference.

## What the offline implementation now exercises

The provider-free acceptance trajectory uses the real twelve-source task and
action validator. It performs:

1. twelve exact two-range acquisition actions;
2. authentic prompt pressure and deterministic relief;
3. treatment-only charged anchored maintenance after actual externalization;
4. an exact evidence-ledger mutation;
5. a deliberately defect-bearing full decision mutation;
6. a mechanical transition into verification;
7. a candidate-bound failing check;
8. a uniquely candidate/artifact/section-bound repair;
9. effect delivery into the following call;
10. a current recheck that passes the frozen mechanical evaluator;
11. submission proposal recording;
12. checkpoint, exact domain/lifecycle hydration, and continuation after the
    first twelve actor calls.

Every actor request exposes the canonical `current_candidate` slot. During
verification the packet also exposes a bounded current-verification slot whose
candidate and check currency are mechanically derived. The treatment scaffold
is present during construction and is demoted before verification work.

The scripted contents are transport fixtures, not model-capability evidence.
They demonstrate that the system can carry a model trajectory through the full
lifecycle without inventing state, duplicating exact bodies, losing candidate
identity, or making the evaluator actor-authoritative.

## Live tranche policy

The first live authorization should cover both fresh configurations for at
most twelve actor calls each, with at most twelve treatment maintenance calls
in total. Each configuration runs in a fresh server process. The system pauses
at the twelve-call checkpoint even when the actor is progressing.

At that pause, Codex reviews the literal transcript rather than asking the host
to decide whether the model is looping. The review records:

- what exact and semantic information was visible;
- what the actor requested and whether demand was novel or repeated;
- each admitted candidate change and what evidence it incorporated or lost;
- each maintenance update, rejection, and induced demand shift;
- effect and check currentness;
- whether the actor is still acquiring productively, constructing, repairing,
  or repeating without progress.

An unchanged continuation may then be separately authorized. Any change to
the policy, action surface, prompt, maintenance cadence, or budgets starts a
new experiment.

## Claim limits

Provider-free qualification does not show that Qwen will construct, verify,
repair, or close. A twelve-call checkpoint is not a task endpoint. A live
difference supports the complete configuration only. Token savings, fewer
reads, or submission are not beneficial unless the exact artifact and
candidate-bound readiness evaluation also improve.

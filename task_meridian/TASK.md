# Authoritative task — Meridian sterile-infusion recovery decision

Meridian Therapeutics must recover production and allocate sterile infusion
bags during a quality hold and regional shortage. Build and maintain the exact
candidate files, then propose submission only when the package is genuinely
ready for independent review.

The world contains sixteen exact, versioned source objects with opaque IDs.
The catalog titles help navigation but do not supply source findings. Read the
bytes needed for the work. Cite sources as `[SOURCE_ID]`.

## Required exact work

Maintain `EVIDENCE_INTEGRATION_LEDGER.md` as a source/version-slot evidence
register. Each slot must remain local to its bound source, preserve important
qualifications and uncertainty, and identify relevant requirements Q01–Q12.
The register is model-authored, lossy, and non-authoritative. It may guide work
but cannot prove readiness.

Construct `BOUNDED_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE_DECISION.md` as a 1,400–2,000-word
Meridian Sterile-Infusion Recovery Decision with exactly the declared section
headings. It must use at least twelve distinct sources and integrate—not merely
list—the governing relationships.

Address all twelve requirement groups:

- Q01 — quality, recall, procurement, execution, and closure authority;
- Q02 — hazard thresholds, lot/line holds, investigation, and release rules;
- Q03 — time-bound demand, overlap, uncertainty, and equitable allocation;
- Q04 — validated manufacturing capacity and shared constraints;
- Q05 — component qualification, supplier timing, inspection, and release;
- Q06 — usable cold-chain distribution, routes, custody, and acknowledgment;
- Q07 — clinical priority, substitution, pharmacy review, and protected cohorts;
- Q08 — qualified staffing, utilities, safety, duty, relief, and continuity;
- Q09 — lot genealogy, facility/patient linkage, privacy, retention, and deletion;
- Q10 — seventy-two-hour execution, owners, sequencing, contingencies, and cost;
- Q11 — candidate-bound sampling, controls, checks, currentness, repair, and recheck;
- Q12 — explicit blockers, falsifiers, independent readiness, and closure evidence.

## Relationship rules

Preserve exact units and distinguish alerts from rejection limits, probabilities
from observed values, targets from observed performance, nominal capacity from
shared validated throughput, registry coverage from demand, dispatch from usable
delivery, and mechanical checking from independent readiness. A change to a
line, sterilizer, component, supplier, route, shift, lot chain, sample plan, or
candidate can make earlier evidence stale.

The decision must state owners, inputs, resources, timings, dependencies,
effects, observations, contingencies, retirement conditions, and evidence that
would falsify important claims. Do not infer that a green format check, source
slot, mutation, or submission establishes safety or completeness.

# Bluehaven Stage 0 red-team disposition

Date: 2026-08-25

Status: offline apparatus finding; no behavioral evidence and no GPU authority

## What is actually being tested

Bluehaven does not isolate a note, prompt, eviction rule, or artifact action.
It compares two complete ways of allocating bounded provider decisions after a
shared authentic pressure history:

```text
B1 batched coupled system
exact ingress → host relief → queued externalizations
→ periodic model integration into exact work
→ effect uptake → construction → current check/repair/recheck/closure

W1 direct-work system
exact ingress → host relief → actor-selected exact work
→ effect uptake → construction → current check/repair/recheck/closure
```

Cadence, semantic ownership, provider cost, task-action opportunity, artifact
anchoring, future evidence demand, verification, and closure are interacting
parts of each policy. A later difference cannot be attributed to a single
component without a separate follow-up.

## Why this task can expose the interaction

The task requires relationships across authority, hazard, demand, treatment,
distribution, continuity, critical care, logistics, verification,
communications, workforce, economics, data, exercise, forecast, and
governance. Several frozen facts are deliberately easy to preserve as words
while corrupting their relationship:

- 0.2 is a chlorine residual in mg/L, not a duration or pressure;
- 38 percent is plume reversal probability, not humidity or demand;
- 94 percent is registry coverage and the remaining six percent is unknown,
  not a demand discount;
- plant ratings share a 15 ML/day East-trunk limit and cannot be added to 21;
- WQ-R7 is a common revision binding, not permission for one arbitrary change;
  and
- an eight-hour fuel contract did not prevent a seventeen-hour observed delay.

This makes artifact persistence two-sided: it can preserve useful integration
or preserve semantic conversion errors. Candidate-bound checking and
independent adjudication are therefore inside the complete feedback system,
not post-hoc decoration.

## Oracle and parity audit

The pressure screen contains no relief, semantic maintenance, digest, note,
progress cue, reentry, or investigator-selected evidence. Both eventual arms
must fork the same sealed model-facing history and pending result. The actor
selects all endogenous reads. Treatment may later use only exact observations
already delivered to its own actor and mechanically externalized under the
common relief rule.

The host may derive only exact identities, sizes, versions, result order,
candidate/check currency, and the three-result maintenance cadence. It may not
infer which source is important, flush a partial semantic batch because work
"seems ready," or add a source that was never acquired.

B1's ordinary actor does not get a second route for replacing the evidence
ledger; B1 maintenance owns that file while the actor owns the decision. W1's
actor owns both files and has no maintenance provider. Giving B1 both semantic
routes would turn the comparison into "maintenance plus direct work" versus
direct work and make accepted-ledger lineage uninterpretable.

## Resource and opportunity audit

The task permits two full 70-line sources per read, so pressure qualification
is based on delivered line coverage and evidence domains rather than result
count. Offline enumeration shows pressure is reachable through legal ingress,
but only the live ordinary trajectory can establish authentic activation.

Both measured policies will receive the same 36-call actor horizon and the
same eight actor calls after the first mechanical construction milestone. B1
may additionally spend at most seven provider calls on its declared
maintenance operation. That asymmetry is intentional and must be reported as
cost: B1 does not obtain task-action slots by consuming maintenance calls, and
W1 is not forced to pay for a provider it does not use. Results will be
reported as a quality/readiness/effect vector plus calls, tokens, cache, and
latency—not as an ungrounded scalar score.

## Evaluator limits

The mechanical evaluator is candidate-bound and catches structural omissions
and named relation conversions. It is not proof that every source statement,
qualification, causal link, or operational recommendation is correct. A model
submission remains a proposal. Independent condition-masked semantic
adjudication must freeze criterion-level findings and closure readiness before
any arm is called useful completion.

When every mechanical gate clears, the actor-visible result may say that the
precheck passed while readiness remains `not_adjudicated`. It does not include
an impossible-to-clear blocker demanding actor-visible independent review.
This preserves a reachable closure proposal while keeping final readiness in
the external evaluation ledger.

## Failure cases that do not permit redesign

The task closes rather than being tuned if the ordinary screen:

- stays below authentic pressure for all 22 calls;
- reaches pressure before the frozen ingress-maturity gate;
- mutates, checks, or submits before the qualifying boundary;
- produces a pending result with no novel source lines; or
- has no strictly positive feasible first-fit substitution.

Likewise, a live maintenance output that exceeds its budget or cites
unobserved sources is rejected once. It is not repaired, retried, or used to
change the batch size after seeing the output.

The 2,400-token B1 admission bound is intentionally larger than Cedar's failed
1,600-token carrier. It was selected before Bluehaven inference from the exact
Cedar output range, so the new interaction is not designed to rediscover a
known transport bottleneck. Its extra residency and generation cost remains a
measured liability of B1.

## Current disposition

Offline Stage 0 qualifies the task and apparatus for exactly one next
operation: the separately authorized 22-call ordinary pressure screen. It does
not authorize the screen, maintenance qualification, or measured comparison.
It supplies no evidence that B1 or W1 is better.

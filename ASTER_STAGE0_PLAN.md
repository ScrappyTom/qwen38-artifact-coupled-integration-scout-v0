Frozen traps include milliseconds versus seconds, per-second versus per-minute
rates, percentages versus probabilities, availability versus payment success,
targets versus observations, component ratings versus shared capacity, sample
coverage versus loss, reporting hours versus minutes, and stale verification
after candidate or source-affecting changes.

## Provenance semantics

Every admitted claim separates:

- owner source and exact version;
- one exact result and an exact complete-line quote inside its delivered range;
- host-resolved line number and host-derived span hash;
- source-reported fact versus source-reported relationship;
- named relationship referents; and
- non-authoritative attribution.

Relationship referents must occur in both the statement and exact owner span.
They do not gain mutation authority. The live maintenance carrier cannot emit a
cross-source derived claim; synthesis belongs in the exact candidate with all
supporting citations. Mechanical provenance never scores predicate, modality,
unit, authority, qualification, or truth.

## Interaction-aligned activation

The common pressure screen uses the ordinary action surface and contains no
semantic provider. Unlike the Meridian acquisition-only gate, prior candidate
effects and current or stale checks do not disqualify a boundary. They are part
of the task trajectory.

The boundary qualifies when:

1. the next exact result would exceed the 20,992-token prompt allowance;
2. at least four fully covered sources across three domains were delivered;
3. the candidate has not been submitted;
4. deterministic positive-savings first-fit relief makes the packet feasible;
5. that relief externalizes at least one previously visible source result; and
6. both W0 and L1 can continue from the same exact pre-treatment fork.

The pending result may be a source observation, candidate effect, or check. The
treatment opportunity is created by the source body that relief externalizes,
not by an acquisition-only host classification.
